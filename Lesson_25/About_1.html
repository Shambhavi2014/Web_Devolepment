Lets answer some key questions.

What are Endangered Flowers?

Endangered flowers are species that are at risk of extinction due to various factors such as habitat loss, climate change, and human activities.

Why should we save Endangered Flowers?

Biodiversity-they contribute to rich diversity on earth and also maintain ecological balence. They also produce a lot of earths oxygen and also are used in many medicies ,meaning less flowers-less cures for various issues.

How can we save Endangered Flowers?

We can save Endangered plants by

Joining our non-profit FlowerBloom Community where you can put donations or come to our nearby setups to save endangered flowers near you! You can also bring snacks and water to help out our members.

If you really want to help save our blessed earth you can do monthly payments to support our community.
