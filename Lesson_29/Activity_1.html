Chinese Cusine

Chinese Cusine is an important part of chine culture which incudes cusine originating from the diverse regions of China,as well as from chinese people in other parts of the world.Because of the chinese dispora and the historical power of the country,Chinese cusine has incluenced many other cusines in Asia , with modifications made to cater to local paletes.Chinese food staples such as rice,soy sauce,tea and tofu and utencils such as chopsticks and woks ,can now be found worldwide.
